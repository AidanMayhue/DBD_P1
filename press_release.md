# Beginner friendly financial asset modeling tool

## Hook
This secondary dataset allows you to find the idea combination of assets to purchase across multiple asset types, increasing diversity 
in investments and minimizing headache.

## Problem Statement
Due to rising cost of living investments are now more important than ever to insure an on time retirement. At the same time,
there is a wide berth of assets to invest in across several asset types, knowing all of them and how they operate in the context of the market takes 
a significant amount of time that may not be available to everyone. Quick and comprehensive knowledge of the market reduces friction from the user
and allows for more accessibility. Additionally, investments can pose significant risk if you are unaware of how to identify risk in an asset.
Many assets are highly volatile and impose risk for long term investors. The specific problem in this instance is identifying low risk investments
across asset types for long term investment.

## Solution Description
This solution collects several features from yahoo finance given a wide range of assets within each asset class over several years. Each asset
class is visualized independently by average return. For more eager users you are given a performance metrics table which allows you to piece together
your own ideal portfolio. Additionally if you are only interested in a specific asset class you are given a table for each asset class. For users who
are more interested in just looking at the highest performers overall there is a list in descending order that shows you the best assets from the 
dataset in terms of pure return.
